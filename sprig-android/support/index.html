Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Sprig Premium screen). Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same Google Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so you cancel them in Google Play, not in the app: open the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. See Google's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Google Play. See Google Play's refund policy.
Where are my notes stored?
On your device, inside the app. Sprig has no account and no cloud sync.
My reminder arrived a few minutes late.
Android may delay reminders to save battery. To get them on time, open Android Settings → Apps → Sprig and allow Alarms & reminders, and turn off battery optimization for Sprig.
I don't get reminder notifications.
Open Android Settings → Apps → Sprig → Notifications and turn notifications on.
I deleted a note by mistake.
Open Settings → Recently Deleted and tap Restore. Deleted notes are kept there for 30 days.
How do I share or save a page?
Open the page and tap the share icon (Export & Share) to save or share it as a PDF, an image or plain text. In the Notes list you can also use a note's ••• menu → Export.
What does Premium add?
The free version includes one notebook plus Quick Notes. Sprig Premium unlocks unlimited notebooks, every premium cover, paper and font, and whole-notebook PDF export. It is an auto-renewing weekly or yearly subscription that starts with a 3-day free trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
