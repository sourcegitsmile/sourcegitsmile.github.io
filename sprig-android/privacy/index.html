Privacy Policy

Sprig: Notes & Sketchbook · Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Sprig: Notes & Sketchbook (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your Android advertising ID, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your notebooks, pages, drawings, photos and settings are stored only on your device. Sprig has no account and no server of its own. Pseudonymous usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can fix problems and improve the app: they are tied only to a random app-instance identifier, never to your name or an account, because Sprig has no sign-in. Your notes, drawings and photos are never sent.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your notebooks, pages, typed text, drawings, checklists, tags and the photos you add to a page.
  • Reminders you set on a note, and your settings such as sort order and appearance.
  • Deleted notebooks and pages stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed.
  • If Android backup is turned on for your Google Account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is made and stored by Google under your account; we cannot see it.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Sprig has no sign-in.
  • Your notes, drawings and photos — they never leave your device.
  • Your Android advertising ID; Sprig does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your precise location. Google Analytics for Firebase sees only an approximate region derived from your IP address (see above); the app never asks for location access.
  • Your contacts or browsing history.
  • Payment details — Premium is bought through Google Play, and we never see your card, Google Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Sprig: Notes & Sketchbook asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in Android Settings.

  • Notifications — to show a reminder you set on a note. Asked only when you first set a reminder; you can say no and still use every other feature.
  • Alarms & reminders — to deliver a note reminder at the time you chose, including after the phone restarts.
  • Photos — to add a picture to a page. Sprig uses the Android photo picker, so it only receives the photo you pick and never your whole library.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Play (in-app purchases) — Processes the Sprig Premium subscription and tells the app whether it is active. Receives: your purchase record, handled by Google (we never see your payment details, Google Account or name). Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether onboarding was finished or the Premium screen was opened) and collects basic diagnostics so we can fix problems and improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity — Sprig has no account, so it is tied only to a random app-instance identifier. Never used for advertising or cross-app tracking; Sprig does not collect your Android advertising ID. Sprig does not use a separate crash-reporting service. Receives: a small set of usage events (never the content of your notes, drawings or photos), a random app-instance identifier, purchase events (product and result), basic diagnostic information such as app version, device model and OS version, locale, and an approximate region derived from your IP address (never your precise location). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period and are not linked to your identity.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete a note or notebook from its ••• menu; it moves to Recently Deleted (Settings → Recently Deleted), where you can delete it for good.
  • Turn notifications off or on in Android Settings → Apps → Sprig → Notifications.
  • Delete all your data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Sprig → Storage — everything lives only on your device.

For any other request, email firehk.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not access your Android advertising ID.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy
