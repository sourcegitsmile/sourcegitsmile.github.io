Privacy Policy

Acrewise · Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Acrewise (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. When you first open Acrewise, iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you tap Allow, Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so we can tell which of our Google Ads brought new users. Google also uses on-device conversion measurement, which matches an ad to an install on your iPhone without sending your name, email or phone number. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your measurements, coordinates, notes, search text and location are never used for this.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your measurements — the points you place, their titles and notes — and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. Acrewise has no account and no server of its own. Map imagery and place search come from Apple Maps, and anonymous usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve the app.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your saved measurements: the points on the map, the area, perimeter or distance, and the title and notes you write.
  • Your settings: area and length units and default map style.
  • PDF reports you export are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to share them.
  • Delete a measurement at any time in Saved Projects, or delete everything by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Acrewise has no sign-in.
  • Your location. Acrewise uses your location only on your iPhone to center the map on you; it is never sent to us or to Google Analytics.
  • Your measurements, coordinates, titles and notes.
  • Payment details — Acrewise Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.
  • Your contacts, photos or browsing history.

3. Device permissions

Acrewise asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Location (While Using the App) — to center the map on where you are so you can measure the land around you. Your location stays on your iPhone. You can say no and still measure by moving the map or searching an address.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — to measure which of our ads brought you to Acrewise. Asked once when you first open the app; the app works the same whether you allow it or not.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — Shows the map and satellite imagery, finds the places and addresses you search for, and draws the map picture used in shared images and PDF reports. Receives: the map area you are viewing and the text you type into the search bar, handled by Apple. Apple does not link this to your Apple Account.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a measurement was finished or the upgrade screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Acrewise, and — only if you allow tracking — tells us which Google Ads campaigns brought new users. Receives: usage events (never your measurements, coordinates, titles, notes, search text or location), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and your advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Acrewise Pro subscription and verifies that it is active. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete any measurement in Saved Projects (swipe left or the ••• menu).
  • Turn tracking off or on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Acrewise.
  • Turn location access off or on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Acrewise.
  • Delete all your data by deleting the app — everything lives only on your iPhone.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
