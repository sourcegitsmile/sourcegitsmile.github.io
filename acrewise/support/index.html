Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the measurements?
Acrewise computes area and distance on the Earth's curved surface, so the maths itself is precise. Accuracy depends on where you place the points and on the quality of the satellite imagery. Results are estimates for planning — they are not a legal survey and should not be used to settle property boundaries.
How do I measure an area?
Tap Area on the map, then tap each corner of the land. The area and perimeter appear as soon as you place the third point. Drag a point to adjust it, tap between two points to add one, and tap Finish to save.
Which units can I use?
Area in acres, square feet, square yards, square meters, hectares, square kilometers and square miles; length in feet, yards, meters, kilometers and miles. Set your defaults in Settings.
What does Acrewise Pro add?
Measuring is free for everyone, and the free plan saves up to 3 projects. Acrewise Pro adds unlimited saved projects and PDF reports you can send to clients. It is a yearly auto-renewing subscription; new subscribers may start with a 3-day free trial.
How do I cancel or restore my subscription?
Cancel any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Acrewise. To restore Pro on a new iPhone or iPad, tap Restore Purchases in Settings with the same Apple Account.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Why doesn't the map center on me?
Location access may be off. Turn it on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Acrewise, or search an address instead.
