Privacy Policy

Reports for Followers Tracker · Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Reports for Followers Tracker (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. When you first open Reports+Followers, iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you tap Allow, Tenjin and Google Analytics for Firebase receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and app installs, the start of a free trial and subscription purchases (product and price) are shared with the ad networks we use so we can tell which of our ads brought new users and subscribers. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected; Apple's SKAdNetwork may still tell an ad network that an ad led to an install, without identifying you or your device. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your Instagram data is never used for this.
  • AI features. Content you choose to submit is sent to OpenAI (AI caption, hashtag and bio writer) for processing — only after you agree on the in-app “Allow AI Processing?” screen.
  • No account, no server of our own. You sign in on Instagram's own login page inside the app. Your Instagram session, follower and following lists, and the reports the app builds from them are stored only on your device; the app talks to Instagram directly from your phone and does not upload these lists to us or anyone else. Reports+Followers has no account and no server of its own. Usage events and crash reports go to Google Firebase, install and purchase events go to Tenjin so we can measure our ads, and — only if you use an AI writer and agree first — the text you type and the photo you pick go to OpenAI.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your Instagram session from signing in on Instagram's own login page. The app never sees or stores your Instagram password.
  • Your follower and following lists, the profiles you track in Ghost Mode, and the reports the app builds from them (unfollowers, who doesn't follow you back, story viewers, admirers).
  • Captions, hashtags and bios you generated.
  • Your settings, including whether you allowed AI processing.
  • Stories, posts and reports you choose to save, which go to your photo library.

What we do not collect:

  • Your Instagram password — you sign in on Instagram's own page.
  • Your follower and following lists or anything else from your Instagram account — they stay on your device.
  • Your name, email address or phone number — there is no account.
  • Your precise location or your contacts.
  • Payment details — subscriptions are bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Reports for Followers Tracker asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Photos — to pick a photo for the AI writers or to repost, and to save stories, posts and exported reports to your photo library.
  • Camera — only if Instagram asks you to verify your identity while you sign in. The camera is used by Instagram's own page; the app does not keep or upload the images.
  • Notifications — to remind you about new results in the app. You can say no and still use every feature.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — to measure which of our ads brought you to the app. See Tracking below. You can say no and still use every feature.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • OpenAI (AI caption, hashtag and bio writer) — Writes the captions, hashtags and bios you ask for. The app sends the request straight to OpenAI; it does not pass through a server of ours. Receives: the text you type and, if you add one, the photo you pick. Shared only with your in-app consent. Their privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — Measures how the app is used, collects crash reports so we can fix problems, and supplies app settings. With your permission it also tells Google which ads led to installs and purchases. Receives: usage and purchase events (product, never your follower lists or Instagram data), a random app-instance identifier, crash reports and basic diagnostic information (app version, device model, OS version), locale and an approximate region derived from your IP address, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Tenjin (ad attribution) — Tells us which of our ads brought each install, trial and subscription, and passes those results to the ad networks we use. Receives: install, session, trial and subscription events (product and price), device model, OS version, locale, country, the app's vendor identifier (IDFV), an Apple Search Ads attribution token, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: tenjin.com/privacy
  • Apple (In-App Purchase) — Processes your subscription and tells the app whether it is active. Receives: your purchase record, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the app. We do not run a server of our own; analytics, crash and attribution data are kept by Google Firebase and Tenjin, and AI requests by OpenAI, under their own retention policies. AI providers process requests to return a result to you and retain request data only as described in their own policies. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For AI features we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Log out of Instagram in Settings; this removes your Instagram session from the app.
  • Delete all your data with Settings → Reset Local Data, or by deleting the app from your device.
  • Change photo, camera and notification access in iOS Settings → Reports+Followers.

For any other request, email smiletechhk.inc@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
