Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Padwell Pro screen, or in Settings). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I start a pad?
On Perform, tap any key. Tap another key to crossfade to it, and tap Stop to fade out. Use Open, Major or Minor to match the song.
The sound stopped during a call or when I unplugged my headphones.
iOS pauses audio for calls and Siri. When it ends, tap a key or Retry on the banner. Unplugging headphones fades the pad out on purpose so it does not blast from the speaker; turn on Keep playing on speaker in Settings if you want it to continue.
How do I send the click to my in-ears and the pads to the main speakers?
Use a 3-conductor (TRS) cable or an audio interface, then turn on Split under Output Layout in Settings (Padwell Pro). The click plays on the left channel and pads and drums on the right.
Does Padwell need the internet?
No. Every sound is built into the app and plays on your device, so it works offline on stage.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Padwell Pro screen or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.
