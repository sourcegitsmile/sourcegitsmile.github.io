Privacy Policy

Padwell: Worship Pads & Drums · Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Padwell: Worship Pads & Drums (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. The first time you open Padwell, iOS asks once whether Padwell may track you (App Tracking Transparency); the question never appears while a pad or click is playing. We advertise Padwell with Google Ads and Apple Search Ads. Only if you tap Allow, Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and app installs, the start of a free trial and Padwell Pro purchases (product and price) are shared with Google Ads so we can tell which of our ads brought new users. Google also uses on-device conversion measurement, which matches an ad to an install on your iPhone or iPad without sending your name, email or phone number. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected; Apple's SKAdNetwork and AdAttributionKit (updated by the Google Analytics for Firebase SDK) may still tell Google Ads that an ad led to an install, without identifying you or your device. Separately, iOS gives Padwell an Apple Search Ads attribution token once after install; it contains no personal data or IDFA and works the same whether or not you allow tracking. You can change your tracking choice any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your songs, set lists and imported audio are never used for this.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your songs, set lists, sound settings, favorites, drum kits and imported samples are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Padwell has no account and no server of its own, and every pad, drum and click is played on your device, so the app works fully offline. Usage events and crash reports go to Google Analytics and Crashlytics for Firebase so we can fix problems and improve the app; only if you allow tracking, they are also used to measure our Google Ads campaigns.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your songs (key, tempo, time signature, pad sound and mix) and set lists in their order.
  • Your sound settings, favorite sounds, drum kits and pad colors, and app settings such as crossfade time, tuning and output layout.
  • Audio files you import from Files into My Samples. Padwell keeps its own copy on your device and never uploads it.
  • All pad sounds and drum kits are built into the app, so they work without an internet connection.
  • Delete a song, a set list or a sample at any time in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Padwell has no sign-in.
  • Your name, email address or phone number for advertising. Google's on-device measurement in Padwell uses no personal information.
  • Audio from your microphone. Padwell does not use the microphone.
  • Your location, contacts, photos or browsing history.
  • Payment details. Padwell Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Padwell: Worship Pads & Drums asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — asked once, at a quiet moment when no sound is playing (never during a song or a set list). If you allow it, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only to measure whether our Google Ads campaigns brought you to Padwell. If you decline, nothing changes in the app and the IDFA is never read.
  • No other permissions — Padwell does not ask for the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, Bluetooth or notifications. Importing a sound uses the system Files picker, which only gives the app the file you choose. A Bluetooth page turner or pedal works as a keyboard and needs no permission.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Padwell Pro subscription or lifetime purchase and verifies that it is active. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Analytics and Crashlytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a pad was started, a set list was performed or the upgrade screen was opened) and collects crash reports, so we can fix problems and improve the app, and — only if you allow tracking — measures whether our Google Ads campaigns led to the app being installed or used, using Google's on-device measurement. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never your songs, set lists, imported audio or anything you type), crash reports and diagnostic data (the code location of a crash, app version, device model, OS version, free memory), an app-instance identifier, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and purchase events (product and result); if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, your advertising identifier (IDFA). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (Apple Search Ads attribution) — Tells us whether an install came from one of our Apple Search Ads campaigns. Receives: an attribution token generated by iOS on your device, sent once after install; it contains no name, email, phone number or advertising identifier (IDFA). Apple returns campaign, ad group and keyword numbers only if the install came from Apple Search Ads. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete a song, set list or imported sample in the app, or delete everything by deleting the app. Your data lives only on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase on the Padwell Pro screen or in Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • Turn haptics off in Settings → Haptics in the app.
  • Allow or stop tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Padwell.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
