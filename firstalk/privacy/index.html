Privacy Policy

Firstalk: English from Zero · Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Firstalk: English from Zero (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your learning progress, review schedule, streak, goals and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Firstalk has no account and no server of its own. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone when your device supports it; otherwise the audio of the sentence you say is sent to Apple to be turned into text, and the app never saves recordings. Pseudonymous usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can fix problems and improve lessons: they are tied only to a random app-instance identifier, never to your name or an account, because Firstalk has no sign-in.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your learning progress, finished lessons, review schedule and streak.
  • Your settings, such as your explanation language, daily goal, reminder time and slow-audio preference.
  • Lesson audio is built into the app, so lessons work without an internet connection.
  • Erase your learning data at any time in Me → Settings → Delete learning data, or by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Firstalk has no sign-in.
  • Voice recordings — the app never saves what you say.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA); Firstalk does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your precise location. Google Analytics for Firebase sees only an approximate region derived from your IP address (see above); the app never asks for location access.
  • Payment details — Firstalk Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.
  • Your contacts, photos or browsing history.

3. Device permissions

Firstalk: English from Zero asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Microphone — to hear the English sentences you say in speaking exercises. The microphone is used only while an exercise is listening, and nothing is recorded or saved. You can say no and keep learning with speaking exercises turned off.
  • Speech Recognition — to turn what you say into text and check it. Recognition runs on your iPhone when your device supports it; otherwise Apple processes the audio (see above).
  • Notifications — to send the study reminder you choose and, if you start a free trial, a reminder before it ends. Nothing leaves your iPhone for this.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Apple Speech Recognition — Turns the English sentence you say in a speaking exercise into text so the app can check it. When your iPhone can recognize English on the device, the audio does not leave your iPhone. Otherwise Apple processes the audio on its servers. Receives: the audio of the sentence you say in a speaking exercise, only when on-device recognition is not available, handled by Apple. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Firstalk Pro subscription and verifies that it is active. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a lesson was finished or the upgrade screen was opened) and collects basic diagnostics so we can fix problems and improve lessons. This data is not linked to your identity — Firstalk has no account, so it is tied only to a random app-instance identifier. Never used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Firstalk does not use a separate crash-reporting service. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never what you say or type), a random app-instance identifier, purchase events (product and result), basic diagnostic information such as app version, device model and OS version, locale, and an approximate region derived from your IP address (never your precise location). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period and are not linked to your identity.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete your learning data in Me → Settings → Delete learning data, or by deleting the app — everything lives only on your iPhone.
  • Turn microphone, speech recognition or notifications off or on in iOS Settings → Firstalk.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
