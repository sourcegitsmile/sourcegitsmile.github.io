Privacy Policy

Sketchlight · Last updated November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Sketchlight (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. After you finish the welcome screens, iOS asks whether Sketchlight may track you (App Tracking Transparency). We advertise Sketchlight with Google Ads. Only if you tap Allow, Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and app installs, the start of a free trial and Sketchlight Pro purchases (product and price) are shared with Google Ads so we can tell which of our ads brought new users. Google also uses on-device conversion measurement, which matches an ad to an install on your iPhone or iPad without sending your name, email or phone number. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected; Apple's SKAdNetwork may still tell Google Ads that an ad led to an install, without identifying you or your device. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your photos, the camera view and your drawings are never used for this.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your photos, sketches, projects, finished drawings, recordings and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. Sketchlight has no account and no server of its own. Turning a photo into lines, finding the subject and finding your sheet of paper all run on your device, and the camera view never leaves it. Usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can fix problems and improve the app; only if you allow tracking, they are also used to measure our Google Ads campaigns.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The photos you choose, the sketches made from them, and your tracing projects with their position and opacity.
  • Photos of your finished drawings and any tracing recordings you make.
  • Your favorite templates, lesson progress, daily challenge streak and settings such as appearance, accent color and reminder time.
  • The template library and lessons are built into the app, so they work without an internet connection.
  • Delete a project or a drawing at any time in My Art, or delete everything by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Sketchlight has no sign-in.
  • Your photos, the camera view or your drawings — they never leave your device unless you share them yourself.
  • Your location, contacts or browsing history.
  • Payment details — Sketchlight Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Sketchlight asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — to show your picture over the paper so you can trace it, and to photograph your finished drawing. The camera view stays on your device and is never uploaded. You can say no and trace in Light Box mode instead.
  • Photos (add only) — to save your sketches and drawings to your photo library when you tap Save to Photos. Choosing a photo uses the system photo picker, so Sketchlight never sees the rest of your library.
  • Notifications — to send the daily challenge reminder at the time you choose. Nothing leaves your device for this.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — asked once after onboarding. If you allow it, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only to measure whether our Google Ads campaigns brought you to Sketchlight. If you decline, nothing changes in the app and the IDFA is never read.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Sketchlight Pro subscription or one-time purchase and verifies that it is active. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a tracing session was started or the upgrade screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve the app, and — only if you allow tracking — measures whether our Google Ads campaigns led to the app being installed or used, using Google's on-device measurement. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never your photos, drawings or anything you type), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and purchase events (product and result); if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, your advertising identifier (IDFA). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete a project or a drawing in My Art, or delete everything by deleting the app — your work lives only on your device.
  • Turn camera, photo saving or notifications off or on in iOS Settings → Sketchlight.
  • Turn the daily reminder off in Settings → Daily challenge in the app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Allow or stop tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Sketchlight.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
