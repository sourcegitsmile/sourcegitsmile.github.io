Privacy Policy

Unfollow Tracker: Followers · Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Unfollow Tracker: Followers (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your Android advertising ID, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • AI features. Content you choose to submit is sent to Anthropic (Claude AI), through our AI service for processing — only after you agree on the in-app “Use AI with Anthropic?” screen.
  • No account; one small server for AI only. You sign in on Instagram's own login page inside the app. Your Instagram session, follower and following lists, and the history the app builds from them are stored only on your device; the app talks to Instagram directly from your phone and never sends these lists to us or anyone else. Two things leave your device: pseudonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase, and — only if you use the AI Caption Writer or AI Hashtag Generator and agree first — the description you type and the photo you add, which go through our AI service to Anthropic.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your Instagram session from signing in on Instagram's own login page. The app never sees or stores your Instagram password.
  • Your follower and following lists, and the history the app builds from them (new followers, unfollowers, who doesn't follow you back).
  • Captions and hashtags generated during the current session.
  • Your settings, including whether you allowed the AI features.
  • Photos or videos you choose to save from Instagram, which go to your device's gallery.

What we do not collect:

  • Your Instagram password — you sign in on Instagram's own page.
  • Your follower and following lists or anything else from your Instagram account — they stay on your device.
  • Your Android advertising ID; the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your precise location. The app never asks for location access, and location data is removed from photos before they are sent to the AI.
  • Your contacts or browsing history.
  • Payment details — Pro is bought through Google Play, and we never see your card, Google Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Unfollow Tracker: Followers asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in Android Settings.

  • Notifications — to send you app notifications. You can say no and still use every feature.
  • Photos — to pick a photo for the AI Caption Writer or AI Hashtag Generator. The app uses the Android photo picker, so it only receives the photo you pick and never your whole library.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI), through our AI service — Writes the captions and hashtags you ask for. Requests pass through our own server, which holds the Anthropic access key and forwards the request; it does not store your photo or text. Receives: the description you type and the photo you add (resized, with location data removed), plus a random app identifier, the app version and a push-notification token used to protect the service from abuse. Shared only with your in-app consent. Their privacy policy: www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play (in-app purchases) — Processes the Pro subscription and tells the app whether it is active. Receives: your purchase record, handled by Google (we never see your payment details, Google Account or name). Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) — Measures how the app is used and collects basic diagnostics so we can fix problems, delivers notifications, and supplies app settings. This data is not linked to your identity or your Instagram account. Never used for advertising or cross-app tracking; the app does not collect your Android advertising ID. Receives: a small set of usage events (never your follower lists, captions or photos), a random app-instance identifier, a push-notification token, purchase events (product and result), basic diagnostic information such as app version, device model and OS version, locale, and an approximate region derived from your IP address (never your precise location). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Our only server is the AI service that forwards caption and hashtag requests to Anthropic; it does not store the text or photo you submit. AI providers process requests to return a result to you and retain request data only as described in their own policies. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period and are not linked to your identity.

6. Legal bases

For AI features we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For analytics and diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn the AI features off in Settings → AI Data Sharing.
  • Log out of Instagram in Settings; this removes your Instagram session and data from the app.
  • Delete all your data in Settings → Delete My Data, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Unfollow Tracker → Storage.
  • Turn notifications off or on in Android Settings → Apps → Unfollow Tracker → Notifications.

For any other request, email firehk.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not access your Android advertising ID.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
