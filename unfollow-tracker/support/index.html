Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings (Restore Purchases) and on the Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same Google Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so you cancel them in Google Play, not in the app: open the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. See Google's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Google Play. See Google Play's refund policy.
Is this an official Instagram app?
No. Unfollow Tracker is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.
Does the app see my Instagram password?
No. You sign in on Instagram's own login page inside the app. The app keeps only the session Instagram gives it, on your device.
Where are my follower lists stored?
Only on your device. The app talks to Instagram directly from your phone and never uploads your lists.
The numbers look out of date.
Pull down to refresh. Instagram limits how often lists can be loaded, so very large accounts may take a while.
How do I turn off the AI features?
Open Settings → AI Data Sharing. Nothing is sent to the AI until you allow it again.
How is my data handled?
See our Privacy Policy.
