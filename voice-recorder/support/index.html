Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in the app's Settings tab). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
The app can't record.
Askmemo needs microphone access. Open iOS Settings → Askmemo and turn on Microphone.
Does recording stop when I lock my phone?
No. Recording continues in the background until you tap Stop.
Where are my recordings stored?
On your device, inside the app. You can also see the audio files in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Askmemo.
Transcribe says Speech Recognition is off.
Open iOS Settings → Askmemo and turn on Speech Recognition.
My language isn't working.
Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition and supports the languages Apple offers on your device. Pick the language before you transcribe. Some languages need an internet connection because Apple transcribes them on its servers.
Why is my summary short or simple?
AI summaries use Apple's on-device model and need an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later). On other devices the app picks the key sentences from the transcript instead.
How does Ask work?
Ask searches the transcripts of your recordings on your device and answers in plain language, with links to the exact moments it used — tap one to play from that point. Only recordings that have a transcript can be searched. Answers need an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later); on other devices the app shows the closest moments instead. Ask is part of Premium.
Ask says there is nothing to ask yet.
Ask only searches recordings that have a transcript. Open a recording and tap Transcribe, or turn on Auto-transcribe new recordings in Settings (Premium).
What does Premium add?
The free version keeps 1 recording. Premium removes the limit and unlocks Transcribe, Summary, Ask and Auto-transcribe. It is a yearly auto-renewing subscription that starts with a 3-day free trial. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged.
I deleted a recording by mistake.
Open Folders → Recently Deleted and tap Restore. Deleted recordings are kept there for 30 days.
