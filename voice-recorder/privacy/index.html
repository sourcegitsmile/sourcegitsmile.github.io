Privacy Policy

Askmemo: Ask Your Recordings · Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Askmemo: Ask Your Recordings (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. When you first open Askmemo, iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). We advertise Askmemo with Google Ads. Only if you tap Allow, Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and app installs, the start of a free trial and subscription purchases (product and price) are shared with Google Ads so we can tell which of our ads brought new users and subscribers. Google also uses on-device conversion measurement, which matches an ad to an install on your device without sending your name, email or phone number. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected; Apple's SKAdNetwork may still tell Google Ads that an ad led to an install, without identifying you or your device. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Your recordings, transcripts and summaries are never used for this.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your recordings, transcripts, summaries, folders and settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. When you ask a question about your recordings, the search and the answer both run on your device. Askmemo has no account and no server of its own. Transcription uses Apple Speech Recognition: when your device cannot recognize the selected language on the device, the audio of that recording is sent to Apple for recognition under Apple's privacy policy. Anonymous usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve the app and measure our ads.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your recordings and their names, markers, folders and favorites. You can also see the audio files in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › Askmemo.
  • Transcripts and summaries. Summaries are created on your device: with Apple's on-device AI model on devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), or with a simple built-in method that picks key sentences on other devices.
  • Questions you ask with Ask and their answers. Ask searches the transcripts of your recordings on your device; on devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), Apple's on-device AI model writes the answer with links to the moments it used, and on other devices the app shows the closest moments instead. Questions and answers are not saved after you leave the Ask screen.
  • Your settings, such as recording quality and appearance.
  • Deleted recordings stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed. Delete everything at any time by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Askmemo has no sign-in.
  • Your recordings, transcripts, summaries, the questions you ask, and the names you give your recordings.
  • Your location, contacts, photos or browsing history.
  • Payment details — Premium is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Askmemo: Ask Your Recordings asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Microphone — to record audio. The microphone is used only while you are recording. Recording can continue in the background so it is not cut off when you lock the screen or switch apps.
  • Speech Recognition — to turn a recording into text. Asked only when you first tap Transcribe; you can say no and still record, edit and play back.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — to measure which of our ads brought you to Askmemo. Asked once when you first open the app; the app works the same whether you allow it or not.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Apple Speech Recognition — Turns a recording into text when you tap Transcribe. When your device can recognize the selected language on the device, the app requires on-device processing and the audio does not leave your device. For other languages, Apple transcribes the audio on its servers. Receives: the audio of the recording you choose to transcribe, only when on-device recognition is not available for that language, handled by Apple. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Premium subscription and verifies that it is active. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a recording was saved, a tool was used or the upgrade screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Askmemo, and — only if you allow tracking — shares installs and purchases with Google Ads so we can tell which of our ads brought new users and subscribers. Receives: usage events (never your recordings, transcripts, summaries, file names or anything you type), an app-instance identifier, purchase events, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, diagnostic data, and your advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete any recording from the list (swipe left or the ••• menu); it moves to Recently Deleted, where you can delete it for good.
  • Turn tracking off or on in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Askmemo.
  • Turn microphone or speech recognition access off or on in iOS Settings → Askmemo.
  • Delete all your data by deleting the app — everything lives only on your iPhone or iPad.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
