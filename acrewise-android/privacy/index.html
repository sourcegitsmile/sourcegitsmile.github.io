Privacy Policy

Acrewise · Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Acrewise (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's Android advertising ID so we can tell which of our ads brought new users. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; once deleted, it is no longer collected. Your measurements, coordinates, notes, search text and precise location are never used for this. Acrewise shows no ads.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your measurements — the points you place, their titles and notes — and your settings are stored only on your device. Acrewise has no account and no server of its own. Map imagery comes from Google Maps, address search uses the Android geocoder, and pseudonymous usage events go to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can improve the app.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your saved measurements: the points on the map, the area, perimeter or distance, and the title and notes you write.
  • Your settings: area and length units and default map style.
  • PDF reports you export are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.
  • Delete a measurement at any time in Saved Projects, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Acrewise has no sign-in.
  • Your precise location. Acrewise uses it only on your device to center the map on you; it is never sent to us or to Google Analytics.
  • Your measurements, coordinates, titles and notes.
  • Payment details — Acrewise Pro is bought through Google Play, and we never see your card, Google Account or name.
  • Your contacts, photos or browsing history.

3. Device permissions

Acrewise asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in Android Settings.

  • Location (while using the app) — to center the map on where you are so you can measure the land around you. Your location stays on your device. You can say no and still measure by moving the map or searching an address.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Maps (Maps SDK for Android) — Shows the map and satellite imagery, and the map picture used in shared images and PDF reports. Receives: the map area you are viewing and basic device information, handled by Google. Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Android geocoder (Google Play services) — Finds the places and addresses you type into Search. Receives: the text you type into the search bar, handled by Google on behalf of your device. Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a measurement was finished or the upgrade screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Acrewise, and tells us which of our ads brought new users. Receives: usage events and purchase events (never your measurements, coordinates, titles, notes, search text or precise location), an app-instance identifier, your Android advertising ID, app version, device model, OS version, locale, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play (in-app purchases) — Processes the Acrewise Pro subscription and tells the app whether it is active. Receives: your purchase record, handled by Google (we never see your payment details, Google Account or name). Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and diagnostic data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete any measurement in Saved Projects.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).
  • Turn location access off or on in Android Settings → Apps → Acrewise → Permissions.
  • Delete all your data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Acrewise → Storage — everything lives only on your device.

For any other request, email hoangdovan.bravo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

Google Analytics for Firebase receives your device's Android advertising ID so we can tell which of our ads brought new users. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads; once deleted, it is no longer collected. Your measurements, coordinates, notes, search text and precise location are never used for this. Acrewise shows no ads.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
