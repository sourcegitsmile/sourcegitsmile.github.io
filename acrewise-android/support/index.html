Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings and on the Acrewise Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in to Google Play with the same Google Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Google Play, so you cancel them in Google Play, not in the app: open the Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. See Google's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Google Play. See Google Play's refund policy.
How accurate are the measurements?
Acrewise computes area and distance on the Earth's curved surface, so the maths itself is precise. Accuracy depends on where you place the points and on the quality of the satellite imagery. Results are estimates for planning — they are not a legal survey and should not be used to settle property boundaries.
How do I measure an area?
Tap Area on the map, then tap each corner of the land. The area and perimeter appear as soon as you place the third point. Drag a point to adjust it, tap between two points to add one, and tap Finish to save.
Which units can I use?
Area in acres, square feet, square yards, square meters, hectares, square kilometers and square miles; length in feet, yards, meters, kilometers and miles. Set your defaults in Settings.
What does Acrewise Pro add?
Measuring area, perimeter and distance is free for everyone. Acrewise Pro unlocks saving your projects and the other Pro tools listed on the upgrade screen. It is an auto-renewing weekly or yearly subscription billed by Google Play; new weekly subscribers may start with a 3-day free trial.
Why doesn't the map center on me?
Location access may be off. Turn it on in Android Settings → Apps → Acrewise → Permissions → Location, or search an address instead.
