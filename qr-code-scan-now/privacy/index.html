Privacy Policy

QR Code Scan Now · Last updated November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information QR Code Scan Now (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. When you first open QR Code Scan Now, iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). We advertise the app with Google Ads. Only if you tap Allow, Tenjin and Google Analytics for Firebase receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and app installs, the start of a free trial and subscription purchases (product and price) are shared with Google Ads so we can tell which of our ads brought new users and subscribers. Google also uses on-device conversion measurement, which matches an ad to an install on your device without sending your name, email or phone number. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, no IDFA is collected; Apple's SKAdNetwork may still tell Google Ads that an ad led to an install, without identifying you or your device. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The contents of the codes you scan are never used for this.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your scan history, the QR codes you create, scanned documents and your settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. QR Code Scan Now has no account and no server of its own. When you open a product barcode, its number is sent to a product database to show the product's name and details. Usage events and crash reports go to Google Firebase, and install and purchase events go to Tenjin, so we can improve the app and measure our ads.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your scan history and the contents of the codes you scan.
  • The QR codes you create.
  • Documents you scan or import, and PDFs you make from them.
  • Your settings, including the theme and the Face ID app lock.

What we do not collect:

  • The contents of the QR codes and barcodes you scan or create.
  • Your documents and photos.
  • Your name, email address or phone number — there is no account.
  • Your precise location. The app asks for location only to show the map when you create a location QR code, and it never sends your location anywhere.
  • Your contacts or browsing history.
  • Payment details — subscriptions are bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

QR Code Scan Now asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — to scan QR codes, barcodes and documents. Images from the camera are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Photos — to import an image that contains a QR code or barcode, and to save QR codes and scanned documents to your photo library.
  • Location (while using the app) — to show your current position on the map when you create a location QR code. You can say no and type a place instead.
  • Face ID — to lock the app if you turn on the app lock. Face ID is checked by iOS; the app never receives your face data.
  • Notifications — to send you app notifications. You can say no and still use every feature.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — to measure which of our ads brought you to the app. See Tracking below. You can say no and still use every feature.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) — Measures how the app is used, collects crash reports so we can fix problems, and delivers notifications. With your permission it also tells Google Ads which ads led to installs and purchases. Receives: usage and purchase events (product and price), a random app-instance identifier, crash reports and basic diagnostic information (app version, device model, OS version), a push-notification token, locale and an approximate region derived from your IP address, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Tenjin (ad attribution) — Tells us which of our ads (for example Google Ads or Apple Search Ads) brought each install, trial and subscription, and passes those results to the ad networks we use. Receives: install, session, trial and purchase events (product and price), device model, OS version, locale, country, the app's vendor identifier (IDFV), an Apple Search Ads attribution token, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Their privacy policy: tenjin.com/privacy
  • Barcode Lookup and USDA FoodData Central (product databases) — Return the name, image and details of a product when you open a product barcode. Receives: the number of the product barcode you open (for example a UPC or EAN), never your scan history or anything else about you. Their privacy policy: www.barcodelookup.com/privacy-policy
  • Apple (In-App Purchase) — Processes your subscription and tells the app whether it is active. Receives: your purchase record, handled by Apple (we never see your payment details, Apple Account or name). Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or remove the app. We do not run a server of our own; analytics, crash and attribution data are kept by Google Firebase and Tenjin under their own retention policies. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for its standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn tracking off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete your scan history inside the app, or delete everything by removing the app from your device.
  • Turn notifications off or on in iOS Settings → Notifications → QR Code.
  • Change camera, photo and location access in iOS Settings → QR Code.

For any other request, email smiletechhk.inc@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
