Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Premium screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
The camera does not scan.
Check that camera access is allowed in iOS Settings → QR Code, hold the code steady inside the frame, and turn on the flashlight in low light.
Where is my scan history stored?
Only on your device. It is not uploaded, so deleting the app also deletes the history.
